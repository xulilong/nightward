## 建筑初稿提示词

Create production GAME ENVIRONMENT SPRITE ATLAS from reference image's exact art style and building fidelity. One square 2048x2048 image, STRICT 2 columns by 2 rows of FOUR SEPARATE isolated full buildings, each entirely contained within its own equal quadrant with generous 8% blank margin on all sides. Solid perfectly flat saturated MAGENTA #ff00ff background everywhere outside objects, also between holes and gaps. No ground plane, no cast shadow outside building base, no text, no people, no sky, no landscape, no frame. Every asset is an exceptionally detailed realistic painterly dark medieval RPG building, matching supplied reference's high-end carved wood, individual weathered slate shingles, irregular stone masonry and restrained bluegreen ivy. CAMERA for all 4: orthographic elevated 50 degrees above horizontal, looking toward the FRONT (south face), minimal yaw 8 degrees, see roof top and front facade; bottom middle of each asset is doorstep, no perspective vanishing. Upper left: intact two-story mossy stone and oak village inn, wide slate gabled roof, small dormer, glowing amber window recesses, hanging lantern at door, ivy walls. Upper right: burnt open barn, charcoal skeletal timber frame with partial slate roof, NO FLAMES OR SMOKE as those will be animated in engine, rubble strictly within its footprint, visible dark interior. Lower left: intact low wide northern granary with bluegray roof, timber awning, oak doors, sacks and barrels tucked against its walls, restrained warm doorway lantern. Lower right: old fortified stone watchhouse, crenellated low upper edge, small timber roof section and weathered dark crimson pennant, deep arch-shaped oak door and aged stone carvings. Keep all four buildings approximately similar rendered width, well separated. Neutral cool diffused daylight with soft roof form shadows and subtle warm windows, readable albedo, NOT dark silhouette, NO overly bright highlights. Surfaces extremely fine and natural, NOT low-poly, NOT minimalist box houses, NOT cartoon. Magenta background perfectly untextured and absent on the objects. These will be cut out with a shader and used as individual scenery objects.

## 建筑布局修正提示词（最终版）

Edit ONLY LAYOUT of this four-building atlas. Preserve the four buildings' appearance, detail and art style. Make exactly 2 columns x 2 rows of equal square cells. Shrink EACH building uniformly to fit completely in its own quadrant with at least 8% cell margin on ALL sides. No building or flag or roof or rubble may extend into another cell. Top left inn, top right burned barn, bottom left granary, bottom right fortified watchhouse. Place entire building's bottom foot at exactly 90% down its cell. ALL space outside buildings and through gaps must remain pure flat MAGENTA #ff00ff, including a wide magenta horizontal and vertical gutter. Preserve full flag atop lower right building and all chimney tops without cropping. NO ground platform, no text, no grid lines. This is a sprite atlas to crop into four equal squares; zero cross-cell overlap is essential.

## 道具提示词

Production game prop sprite sheet, same exquisitely detailed realistic painterly medieval forest-village RPG art as reference. Square canvas. EXACT two columns and three rows = six cells, each asset strictly contained inside its own cell, at least 12 percent SOLID PURE MAGENTA #ff00ff MARGIN around each prop, no asset touches any cell edge. Completely flat magenta background, including holes. NO labels, no text, no people, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no frame. Orthographic elevated 50 degree overhead gameplay camera looking north at south front, slight 8 degree yaw, consistent for all props. Row1 left: ancient circular stone well with wooden posts and a blue-gray slate canopy, bucket and rope, moss. Row1 right: abandoned oak handcart with delicate wooden spokes and two burlap sacks, damaged shaft. Row2 left: short broad ruined moss-covered stone garden wall with one taller carved stone end pillar and an amber hanging brass lantern, ferns at base. Row2 right: iron prisoner cage with wooden platform and weathered bars, door visibly closed, no person inside. Row3 left: stores of grain in six burlap sacks, two oak barrels and one wooden crate, a low weathered awning behind them, small compact composition. Row3 right: dense low patch of woodland ferns, ivy, roots, mossy rocks and tiny ivory wildflowers. All assets completely visible and isolated. Individual slate flakes, carved stone, subtle weathering and natural irregularities at high detail; textured original dark fantasy game art, NOT low poly, NOT cartoon, not photographic cutouts. Soft cool neutral light, subtle warm lantern only.

## 地面提示词

Create a seamless tileable square ground material texture for the rainy medieval village in the reference. Only flat ground texture fills the entire image. Exact top-down orthographic camera, NO perspective, NO buildings, NO plants taller than ground, NO objects, NO text. Irregular small worn bluegray cobblestones and thin fractured flagstones arranged organically, varied sizes roughly 10 to 18 centimeters, about 15 stones across image width. Thin dark earthen grout, very restrained moss in scattered joints, worn rounded edges, small shallow wet patches. Match fine realistic painterly game material fidelity of reference. Neutral diffuse lighting, no cast shadows, no directional highlights or bright reflections baked in; surface visibly detailed but low contrast and uncluttered to keep hero and combat readable. Dominant natural muted gray stone with slight cool teal and brown tint, not emerald green, not uniformly black, not regular rectangular grid. Flat albedo material suitable for texture repetition, edges seamlessly repeat on both axes.

实际生成文件均为 1254 × 1254；代码按实际尺寸计算图集区域与落地锚点。概念图不是实机截图，当前实现采用分层 2.5D 场景和 3D 动态角色。
